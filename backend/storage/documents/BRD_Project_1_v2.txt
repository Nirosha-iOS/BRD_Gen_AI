**Business Requirement Document**

**Overview**

Project Background:
The client has approached us to develop a mobile app that enables customers to easily order drinking water with various features such as selecting water type, quantity, and delivery time, among others.

Business Context:
The rise of e-commerce and online ordering has led to increased demand for convenient services. The client aims to capitalize on this trend by providing a seamless experience for its customers to purchase drinking water.

Purpose of the Document:
This Business Requirement Document (BRD) outlines the functional and non-functional requirements, risks, assumptions, and scope of the mobile app project. It serves as a comprehensive guide for stakeholders, developers, and testers to ensure that the final product meets the client's expectations.

**Objective**

Primary Objectives:

* Develop a user-friendly mobile app that enables customers to easily order drinking water
* Provide a seamless experience for one-time and subscription-based orders
* Offer online payment and cash on delivery options

Success Criteria:
The project is considered successful if it meets the following criteria:

* The app is launched within the scheduled timeline
* Customer satisfaction ratings exceed 90%
* The app achieves a minimum of 1000 downloads within the first three months

Business Value:
By developing this mobile app, the client aims to increase revenue by up to 20%, enhance customer loyalty, and reduce the operational costs associated with manual ordering processes.

**Scope**

In Scope:

* Mobile app development for both iOS and Android platforms
* User registration and login features
* Order management system (including one-time and subscription-based orders)
* Payment gateway integration (online payment and cash on delivery options)
* Delivery agent management system
* Admin dashboard for order, price, and customer management
* Notifications for order and delivery updates

Out of Scope:

* Integration with third-party logistics providers
* Development of a web application or desktop software

Scope Boundaries:
The project scope only includes the development of the mobile app, its features, and the associated backend infrastructure. Any additional requirements beyond this scope will be evaluated and approved by the stakeholders.

**Functional Requirements**

Detailed Functional Requirements:

1. User Registration: Users can register for an account using their email address and password.
2. Order Management:
	* Customers can select water type, quantity, and delivery time for one-time orders.
	* Subscription-based orders allow customers to set recurring deliveries with customizable frequencies.
	* Orders are subject to availability and confirmation by the admin team.
3. Payment Gateway Integration:
	* Online payment options include credit cards, e-wallets, and bank transfers.
	* Cash on delivery option available for select areas.
4. Delivery Agent Management:
	* Agents can receive and update delivery status in real-time.
5. Admin Dashboard:
	* Order management, including order history and tracking.
	* Price management (including promotions and discounts).
	* Customer management (including profile updates).

User Stories:

1. As a customer, I want to easily select water type, quantity, and delivery time for my one-time order so that I can receive my desired product promptly.
2. As a subscription-based customer, I want to set recurring deliveries with customizable frequencies so that I can enjoy my favorite drinking water regularly.

Use Cases:

1. Order Placement: A customer places an order for a specific type of drinking water with a selected quantity and delivery time.
2. Payment Processing: The payment gateway processes the customer's transaction, and the app updates the order status accordingly.
3. Delivery Status Update: A delivery agent receives and updates the delivery status in real-time.

Business Rules:

1. Orders are subject to availability and confirmation by the admin team.
2. Subscription-based orders require a minimum commitment period of three months.
3. Cash on delivery option is only available for select areas.

**Non-Functional Requirements (NFR)**

Performance Requirements:

* The app should respond within 2 seconds for 90% of user interactions.
* The average order processing time should not exceed 30 minutes.

Security Requirements:

* Data encryption for sensitive information (e.g., payment details).
* Secure authentication and authorization mechanisms.

Scalability Requirements:

* The system should be designed to handle a minimum of 1000 concurrent users during peak hours.
* Database design should ensure efficient data retrieval and storage.

Usability Requirements:

* The app's user interface should be intuitive, with minimal learning curve.
* Users should be able to navigate the app without encountering any major issues.

Compliance Requirements:

* Payment gateway integration must comply with relevant industry standards (e.g., PCI-DSS).
* Data collection and storage must adhere to applicable data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR).

**Risks**

Technical Risks:

1. Mobile app development delays due to unexpected technical challenges.
2. Integration issues with the payment gateway or other third-party services.

Business Risks:

1. Market saturation, making it difficult for the client's brand to stand out.
2. Competition from established players in the e-commerce and drinking water industries.

Mitigation Strategies:

1. Technical risks: Implement a phased development approach, conducting thorough testing and quality assurance at each stage.
2. Business risks: Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify areas for differentiation; develop a strong marketing strategy to promote the client's brand.

**Assumptions**

Key Assumptions:

* The client will provide necessary resources (e.g., infrastructure, personnel) to support the project.
* The development team will have access to relevant documentation and stakeholder input throughout the project lifecycle.

Dependencies:

1. The payment gateway integration is dependent on the availability of the chosen payment processor's APIs.
2. The app's scalability requirements are dependent on the expected user base and usage patterns.
